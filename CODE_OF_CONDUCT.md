# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on ideas and evidence.
- Respect privacy and never disclose another person's confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [karerckor GitHub profile](https://github.com/karerckor). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and public representation of the project.
